Welcome

Welcome Titans players, families & fans.

Eye Atelier Eyewear is the proud optical partner of the NJ Titans, Middletown Sports Complex, and Sparks Day Camp — we welcome players, coaches, staff, and their families. We bring you the latest in optical fashion, sports eyewear, and brand-name lenses.

Whether it's your first pair of eyeglasses or you're looking for a safety sport pair for playing hockey, we're the place that can make both happen. We specialize in prescription sunglasses and all the latest lens technologies. We can fill your prescription, or you can be seen by our doctor — we can even copy the prescription in your current glasses and duplicate it perfectly.

We look forward to seeing you.

Premium Lens Technology

Built for how you use your eyes.

Every recommendation is personalized — to your prescription, your position on the ice or off it, and how you actually spend your day. No two fittings are the same.

01

Varilux

Progressive lenses engineered for seamless, natural vision at every distance — no visible line, no adjustment period that gets in your way.

02

Crizal

Anti-reflective, scratch-resistant coatings that keep lenses clear under rink lights, headlights, and everyday glare.

03

Transitions

Lenses that adapt automatically from indoor rink to outdoor daylight, so one pair keeps up with a full day's schedule.

04

Trivex Lenses

Built with safety and durability in mind, Trivex lenses are not only impact resistant and offer the highest optical quality — they are also the lightest material available, perfect for your athlete or anyone looking for lightweight comfort.
